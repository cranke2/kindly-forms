Questions

Does Mum need a diagnosis?

No. It's about the help she needs because of an illness, disability or mental health condition, including frailty, dementia, sight or hearing loss, not about the name of the condition.

She manages on her own. Can she still get it?

Yes, possibly. What counts is the help she needs, even if nobody is giving it, and even if she struggles through on her own, slowly or unsafely.

Will it reduce her pension or other benefits?

No. It isn't means-tested or taxed and it doesn't reduce other benefits. It can actually increase Pension Credit, Housing Benefit or Council Tax Reduction, and may let a family carer claim Carer's Allowance.

Does she have to spend it on care?

No. It can be spent on anything, such as taxis, heating, a cleaner or meals.

Where does this apply?

England, Wales and Northern Ireland. In Scotland it has been replaced by Pension Age Disability Payment, which Social Security Scotland runs.
